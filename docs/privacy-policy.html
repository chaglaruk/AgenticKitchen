Agentic Kitchen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Agentic Kitchen accesses, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the Android application.

1. Information stored on your device

Agentic Kitchen is designed as a local-first application. Pantry inventory, quantities, storage locations, expiry information, cooking sessions, cooking history, recipe-related state, and app preferences are primarily stored locally on your device.

If you choose to provide your own Gemini API key, Agentic Kitchen stores that credential using Android Keystore-backed authenticated encryption. The application does not intentionally store the API key in plaintext after a successful secure migration.

2. AI requests and information sent to service providers

Some features can use artificial intelligence. Depending on the provider you select, information required to perform a requested AI task may be transmitted to Google services.

  • Managed AI: Agentic Kitchen can use Firebase AI Logic to send the information needed for the requested task to Google's Gemini services.
  • Bring your own key: if you choose the direct Gemini option, requests are sent to the Google Gemini API using the API credential you provide.
  • Offline fallback: supported deterministic features can operate without sending an AI request.

AI requests may include information that you intentionally provide for the task, such as ingredient names, pantry context, recipe text, cooking questions, preferences relevant to the request, or an image you explicitly choose for analysis.

3. Images and camera use

Agentic Kitchen may request camera access for optional image-based ingredient or cooking assistance. An image is transmitted to the active AI provider only when you choose to use an image-analysis feature and proceed through the relevant disclosure or confirmation flow.

Agentic Kitchen does not intentionally persist selected vision images in its application database or private files after analysis. AI results can be inaccurate and must be reviewed by the user.

4. Firebase App Check, Play Integrity, and configuration services

Agentic Kitchen uses Firebase App Check and Google Play Integrity for abuse prevention and to help verify that requests originate from a legitimate application installation. These services may process application, installation, device-integrity, and security-related signals or tokens as required to provide those protections.

Firebase Remote Config may be used to retrieve non-secret application configuration such as model-selection settings. Agentic Kitchen does not intentionally include Firebase Analytics, advertising SDKs, Firebase Authentication, Firestore, or Firebase Storage in the current core architecture.

5. Logging

Release logging is designed not to include API credentials, prompts, AI responses, pantry contents, user questions, image content, absolute file paths, or device identifiers. Debug diagnostics are intended to contain only limited technical metadata needed for development and troubleshooting.

6. Advertising and sale of data

Agentic Kitchen does not currently contain banner or interstitial advertising and does not sell personal data.

7. Data retention and deletion

Locally stored Agentic Kitchen data remains on your device until you remove it through available application controls, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application, subject to normal Android device behaviour.

Credentials stored by Agentic Kitchen can be removed by removing or replacing the relevant provider credential in the application.

Information transmitted to Google or another service provider is processed under that provider's applicable terms, privacy policy, and retention practices. Agentic Kitchen does not control a third-party provider's independent retention obligations.

8. Security

Agentic Kitchen uses reasonable technical safeguards appropriate to the application, including Android Keystore-backed encryption for supported credentials, disabled Android application backup for sensitive application data, and validation controls around structured AI output. No method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Children's privacy

Agentic Kitchen is not designed as a child-directed service. The application should not be used to replace adult judgement regarding allergens, food safety, cooking temperatures, or food doneness.

10. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when Agentic Kitchen's features, service providers, or data practices change. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
